\# 📊 Executive Sales Performance Dashboard



\## 📌 Project Overview



This project presents an interactive \*\*Executive Sales Performance Dashboard\*\* built in \*\*Power BI\*\* to help business leaders monitor sales performance, profitability, customer trends, and regional performance.



The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, and drill-down analysis, enabling faster and more informed decision-making.

\## 🎯 Business Objectives



\- Monitor overall sales performance

\- Analyze revenue and profit trends

\- Evaluate product category performance

\- Identify high-performing regions

\- Understand customer segment behavior

\- Support executive decision-making using interactive dashboards

\---



\## 📈 Business Insights



\- The East region generated the highest overall revenue.

\- Technology emerged as the strongest-performing product category.

\- Furniture delivered the highest profit contribution.

\- The Consumer segment accounted for the largest share of revenue.

\- Sales peaked during July, indicating seasonal demand patterns.
